How the Mouse-Over Flyouts Work in This UI:
When you collapse the menu bar, traditional menus hide their sub-features completely. In this premium design, closing the menu gives you the best of both worlds:
1
Zero Lost Features
Hovering over Billing & Orders reveals Tax Invoices, Quotations, Proforma, and POs with their live count badges. You never have to reopen the menu just to reach a sub-page.
2
Smooth Hover Bridge
An invisible 20px physics bridge connects the icon to the flyout card, so your mouse moves smoothly without the menu flickering or closing prematurely.
3
1-Click Direct Launch
Clicking any item inside the mouse-over flyout instantly navigates to that feature and updates the active tab seamlessly.
